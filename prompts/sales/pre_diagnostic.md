# Prompt: Pre-Diagnostic Draft

## Purpose
Create a short, evidence-based pre-diagnostic before proposal.

## Required sections
1. Context
2. What is visible now
3. Core weak point
4. Business impact
5. 2-3 solution levels
6. Questions for call
7. Recommended next step

## Guardrails
- Do not invent facts.
- Do not include pricing.
- Do not convert to a full proposal.
