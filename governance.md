# Governance and Quality Controls

## 1. Human approval gates

Mandatory approval before sending:
- first-touch outreach,
- pre-diagnostic reports,
- proposals,
- sensitive follow-up after objections.

## 2. Prompt change policy

Change prompts only when at least one condition is true:
- measurable KPI drop,
- repeated failure pattern,
- validated segment shift,
- compliance or tone risk.

## 3. Anti-generic policy

Any output fails if it has:
- vague business claims,
- no event anchor,
- no next-step request,
- broad "we help companies" language.

## 4. Data quality controls

Each opportunity card must include:
- source,
- company,
- trigger event,
- confidence,
- missing data list,
- current stage.

## 5. Review ritual

- weekly 30 min quality review,
- monthly architecture review,
- quarterly offer ladder and segment review.
